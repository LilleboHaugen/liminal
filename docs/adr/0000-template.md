# ADR-NNNN: <Decision in a few words>

## Context

The problem, the forces and constraints involved, and the facts (with measurements or sources). Why a
decision is needed now.

## Decision

What we will do, stated normatively (MUST / SHOULD / MAY). Specific enough that compliance can be
checked.

## Alternatives considered

For each: what it is, why it was not chosen. Always consider "build it ourselves" and "do nothing".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, costs, risks, and what would make us revisit this decision.

## Verification

How compliance is checked (lint, xtask check, test, review item).
