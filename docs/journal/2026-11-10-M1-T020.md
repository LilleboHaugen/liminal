## Decisions
- `#![deny(unsafe_code)]` instead of `forbid`: the crate has `unsafe = "allow"` and M1-T022 must
  be able to allow it in its SIMD modules.
- `core::arch` entries in `clippy.toml` use `allow-invalid = true` (each path exists on one
  architecture only). Verified: clippy 0.1.99 accepts the key and reports nothing.
- `Mat4::from_scale_rotation_translation` is built through `Affine3`, so MATH-R047 and the
  same-bits clause of MATH-R090 hold by construction.
- `Mat4::determinant`/`inverse` use the Laplace expansion over 2×2 minors of rows 0–1 and 2–3;
  the spec fixes no expression for them beyond the integer exactness of MATH-R044.
