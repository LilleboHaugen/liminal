# 2026-11-09 — ADR-0026: Sub-agents for task work (proposed)

**Session type:** planning
**Tasks touched:** none. The owner limited this session to the rule change: no review or
implementation of M1 tasks.

## Done
- The owner asked for a rule change: sessions may spawn sub-agents for task work, and each
  sub-agent meets every requirement of an owner-started session. Wrote
  `docs/adr/0026-sub-agents-for-task-work.md` (`proposed`). It lists the five risks from ADR-0020's
  Context and Alternatives (R1–R5) and the decision that answers each. It covers: standing permission
  (no per-session opt-in), equivalence, parent-only orchestration, worktree/branch/crate isolation, review
  independence (a parent and its sub-agents count as one session; fixed review prompt), serial
  merges by the reviewer, installs relayed through the parent, traceability, limits (3
  concurrent, at most 1 review; at most 3 + 3 per session; no nesting) and task choice.
- The owner's conditional approval is recorded in the ADR.
- `docs/adr/README.md`: row 0026, `proposed`. `docs/STATUS_NOTES.md`: next step is the ADR review.
- The dependent edits (AGENTS.md step 6, definition of done, "Changing the rules"; WORKFLOW.md §2,
  §7, §8; REVIEW.md intro and item 10; journal README) are written out verbatim in the ADR under
  "Edits on acceptance". They are **not** applied.

## Decisions
- `supersedes: []` while `proposed`, against the owner's literal request
  (`supersedes: [ADR-0020]`). XTASK-R021 requires symmetric links and `superseded` iff
  `superseded_by` is set. Filling the field would mark ADR-0020 superseded before ADR-0026 is in
  force. The ADR states the supersession in its body. Acceptance step 1 sets both fields.
- AGENTS.md, WORKFLOW.md and REVIEW.md left unchanged. A conditional rule in AGENTS.md is itself
  a rule change without an accepted ADR, and it contradicts step 6 as written. The process docs
  are binding in the same way. All wording is in the ADR, as the owner allowed.
- Branched from `main`, not from `task/M1-T002-check-docs-follow-ups`, so that merging the ADR
  does not bring unreviewed M1-T002 work to `main`.
- The first draft required the owner to opt in per session (R1). The owner rejected that: sessions
  spawn sub-agents by default under the other rules, to speed up work. Changed in the ADR
  (Decision 1, Alternatives, Owner approval). The owner can still forbid or limit sub-agents in a
  session. Chains (R4) stay bounded by the per-session totals.
- Additions beyond the owner's list, all of them stricter: the parent does no task work; a total per-session limit as well as a concurrency
  limit (R4); a verbatim review prompt template, recorded in the journal (R3); sub-agents never
  install (rule 16 needs the owner's first-hand yes); ADRs that change rules are reviewed only
  in owner-started sessions.
